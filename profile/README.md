# Bloccpay

**Cross-border payroll & invoicing — pay global talent in their local currency, in minutes.**

Bloccpay is a payroll and invoicing platform for global businesses and the contractors and teams they work with. Businesses fund payroll in familiar currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, and more) and pay talent across regions simultaneously; talent receives payment in their local currency, to their bank account, in minutes — no crypto knowledge required. Stablecoin settlement runs invisibly under the hood, making transfers instant, low-cost, and borderless. We focus on the corridors where traditional payment infrastructure fails remote workers — high fees, long delays, and value lost in transit.
